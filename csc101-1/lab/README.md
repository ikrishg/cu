# Wildlife Census

These labs share one domain: **wildlife census and conservation** — counting animals, judging how healthy a population looks, and deciding what that means for a park or reserve.

A typical program asks for things like reserve codes, births and deaths, habitat area, or sightings at checkpoints, then reports density, conservation status (stable through critically endangered), or a short survey summary. Later labs keep that same world while bringing in arrays, strings, functions, pointers, and structures so the “data” feels like real census records instead of bare numbers.

The through-line is a small conservation desk: take field numbers in, make sense of them, and leave with a status you could act on.
