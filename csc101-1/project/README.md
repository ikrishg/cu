# Kwish Swiss Bank

A small console banking system built as the CSC101-1 group project — **Kwish Swiss Bank**, styled as a kiosk-style counter for opening accounts and day-to-day banking.

You start by opening a **savings** or **current** account (18+ only). From there you can deposit and withdraw, keep an eye on a ₹500 minimum balance, run simple or compound interest on a principal (including the live balance), and review a short history of what happened in the session.

The idea sits somewhere between a classroom exercise in structured C and a sketch of kiosk banking: one customer session, clear menus, and the usual ledger moves without a real backend.
